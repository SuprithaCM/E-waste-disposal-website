BEFORE YOU HAND IT OVER

Choose a responsible recycler.

01

Verify credentials

Check current authorization, accepted materials and local rules with the relevant pollution control authority.

02

Ask about data

Erase personal data from phones and computers and confirm how storage devices will be handled.

03

Confirm rates

Ask the collector for a current quote and weighing method before agreeing to a sale.
