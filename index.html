START WITH THE BASICS

What counts as e-waste?

View all categories →

E-waste includes electrical or electronic products that are unwanted, broken, or at the end of their useful life. Some contain valuable materials; others can release harmful substances if handled incorrectly.

▭

Rechargeable batteries, UPS units and power equipment.

WHY IT MATTERS

Better disposal protects more than space.

01

Soil & water

Unsafe dumping can allow lead, mercury and other hazardous substances to contaminate soil and water.

02

Air quality

Burning wires or electronics to recover metals can release toxic smoke and fine particles.

03

Resources

Recycling can recover metals and materials, reducing the need to extract some new raw resources.
